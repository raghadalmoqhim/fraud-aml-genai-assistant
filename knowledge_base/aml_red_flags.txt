AML Transaction Red Flags

1. Unusual Transaction Patterns
Transactions that are irregular, unusual, or inconsistent with expected customer behavior may require further review.

2. Unusual Transaction Size or Frequency
Transactions with an amount or frequency that does not have a clear or logical explanation may indicate elevated risk.

3. Customer Behavior
Activity that differs significantly from the customer's expected profile or normal transaction behavior may require investigation.

4. Geographic Risk
Transactions involving jurisdictions with higher money laundering or financial crime risk may require enhanced review.

5. Source of Funds
Unclear, inconsistent, or suspicious sources of funds or wealth may represent an AML risk indicator.

6. Multiple Indicators
A single red flag does not necessarily indicate money laundering. Multiple indicators, transaction context, and customer behavior should be considered together.

7. Risk-Based Review
Higher-risk activity should receive greater attention and investigation based on the institution's risk-based approach.
